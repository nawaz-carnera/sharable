Here's the complete flow of EōS Fitness in crisp one-liners:

---

**🏢 What & Who**
- Founded in 2014 by Rich Drengberg, former VP of Sales at Gold's Gym
- Headquartered in Dallas, Texas
- Operates 200+ company-owned gyms across 7 US states
- Serves 1.5 million+ members with ~9,400 employees

---

**🎯 The Problem They Solve**
- Cheap gyms were too bare-bones; premium gyms were too expensive
- Scientists… wait — *scientists spend time on admin* — that's Prendio. EōS solves the fitness access gap: quality experience at an accessible price
- The mid-tier ($40–60/mo) offered neither the savings nor the experience to justify itself

---

**💡 The Core Idea**
- Registered positioning: **"High Value. Low Price.®"** — abbreviated HVLP
- Big-format gyms (35,000–50,000 sq ft) spread fixed costs across thousands of members
- Every gym is company-owned — no franchising — ensuring consistent quality

---

**💰 How They Make Money**
- Three membership tiers: Will Do ($9.99), Will Crush ($24.99), Will Power ($29.99)
- Add-ons: Personal Training, Kids' Club, VIP Guest, retail merchandise
- Annual fee ($59.99) + processing fee ($2.99/mo) stack on top of base subscriptions

---

**🏋️ What Makes Them Different (Amenities)**
- Free weights up to 150 lbs — capturing the market Planet Fitness deliberately excludes
- MOVEōS Cinema — cardio in a movie theater, a signature-only-at-EōS feature
- The EōS Yard® — indoor turf functional training zone
- Indoor pool, basketball courts, sauna — closing the gap with premium clubs
- Refresh / The Tank — cold plunge, hot tub, infrared sauna for recovery
- The Set — content creation studio targeting Gen Z influencers
- EGYM smart strength equipment — connected fitness at HVLP pricing

---

**🔄 How They Retain Members**
- Welcome Workout: free 1-hour PT session converts new members into PT buyers
- G-Fit classes and proprietary formats (BBL, FU Studio) build sticky habits
- 24/7 access at most locations removes scheduling friction
- ~91% member retention rate — among the highest in the HVLP segment

---

**📈 Growth Story**
- 40 gyms in Jan 2020 → doubled to 80 by May 2023 → 100th gym milestone → 200+ by 2025
- October 2025: acquired 23 Gold's Gym SoCal locations overnight, growing by ~20%
- Southern California is now their largest single market with 46 locations
- Target: 250 gyms by 2030

---

**💼 Who Owns It**
- BRS & Co. backed EōS for 11 years (2014–2025)
- May 2025: TSG Consumer Partners acquired EōS at ~$1.5B valuation
- TSG previously scaled Planet Fitness from ~400 → 1,500+ locations — same playbook
- Drengberg stays as CEO and retains equity stake

---

**⚔️ Competitive Position**
- Planet Fitness: 2,700+ locations but thinner amenities — now upgrading to catch up
- Crunch Fitness: closest rival, 500+ locations, opened 91 new gyms in 2025 alone
- LA Fitness / 24 Hour Fitness: mid-tier chains being squeezed from below by HVLP 2.0
- EōS's edge: more amenities than Planet Fitness, lower price than LA Fitness, fully company-owned unlike Crunch

---

**📊 Market Tailwinds**
- HVLP gyms saw 3.8% visit growth in H1 2025 — best start on record
- 38% of Gen Z hold gym memberships vs. 25% average — structural demand tailwind
- US fitness club market projected to grow from $45.8B (2025) to $71.5B by 2035

---

**⚠️ Risks to Watch**
- Heavy debt (~$650M assumed) makes them sensitive to interest rate environment
- Crunch + PureGym are flush with PE capital and expanding fast
- PE ownership historically pressures cost-cutting that erodes member experience
- Geographic concentration in Sun Belt — Northeast and Midwest largely untapped

---

**🔮 The Big Bet**
- Can EōS maintain a premium in-gym culture while growing 20%+ annually under PE ownership?
- TSG has $500M reportedly earmarked for further acquisitions — Midwest/Northeast roll-ups expected
- If the Planet Fitness playbook repeats: EōS could realistically target 1,000+ locations long-term
